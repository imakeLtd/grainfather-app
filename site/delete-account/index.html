Delete your Grainfather account

This page is for the Grainfather app (called Grainfather Community on the App Store), published by BSGi NZ Limited. You can delete your account and its data yourself in the app, or ask us to do it for you.

In the app

  1. Open the Grainfather app and sign in.
  2. Go to the More tab and tap your name at the top.
  3. On the Account page, tap Delete Account at the bottom.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

You need an internet connection. When it's done, the app signs you out.

Without the app

Send us a request through the Grainfather Help Centre. Use the email address you sign in with, and say you'd like your app account deleted.

Contact Grainfather support

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your Grainfather account and the data you keep in it: your profile, recipes, brew sessions and equipment. This can't be undone.

Messages you've sent our support team are kept by our help desk.
